Send to CloudCut

Send flat parts from your Fusion design to CloudCut to make CNC toolpaths, or save them as JSON or SVG files.

Where to find it

The Send to CloudCut button appears in three places:

  • Design workspace, Solid tab, Create panel
  • Design workspace, Utilities tab, Add-ins panel
  • Manufacture workspace, Actions panel

Sending parts

  1. Select the bodies you want to cut, then click Send to CloudCut. You can also pick bodies once the dialog is open.
  2. Check the Name. It is used for the CloudCut job names and saved file names.
  3. Choose Output Units and leave Output set to Send to CloudCut.
  4. Click OK. Your browser opens CloudCut; sign in to claim the parts. You get one job per material thickness.

Dialog options

Bodies
The parts to export. Each should be a flat, extruded part: the exporter reads its planar faces for outlines, holes and pockets.
Output
Send to CloudCut uploads the parts. Save JSON file and Save SVG file save one file per thickness on your computer instead, plus a _debug.txt report.
Appearances / Thicknesses
Untick an appearance or thickness to leave those bodies out of the export.
Privacy tab
A link to CloudCut's privacy policy.

What is sent

Nothing leaves your computer until you click OK with Send to CloudCut selected. Then the add-in uploads the geometry of the parts you selected, the name you entered, and the add-in's name and version. You do not need to be signed in to send; the upload is linked to your account only when you open the link and sign in. Unclaimed uploads are deleted after about 7 days. See the privacy policy for details (a copy is included with the add-in).

Troubleshooting

"No exportable geometry found"
The selected bodies have no flat faces to read. Make sure they are extruded flat parts, not curved or sculpted bodies.
"Couldn't reach CloudCut"
Check your internet connection. When asked, you can save the export as JSON files instead and import them in CloudCut later.
Two Send to CloudCut buttons
Both CloudCut (from the Autodesk App Store) and CloudCut Direct (downloaded straight from CloudCut) are installed. You only need one; turn the other off in Utilities > Add-ins.
Turning the add-in off
Open Utilities > Add-ins, select CloudCut and click Stop. Clear Run on Startup to keep it off.
